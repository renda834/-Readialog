# Reading preference rubric

Use the following scale bands in order. Base each choice on evidence in the interview.

| Metric | 0–20 | 21–40 | 41–60 | 61–80 | 81–100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prose density | spare language | direct style with some ornament | flexibility about density | sustained layers of literary detail | highly demanding prose |
| Pacing | extended contemplation | gradual development | both movement and reflection | quick narrative momentum | near-continuous action |
| Tone | tragedy and bleakness | seriousness with some warmth | a mix of light and dark | predominantly optimistic | comic or playful |
| Worldbuilding | setting stays in the background | light scene setting | detail that makes the plot work | a richly realized setting | interest in elaborate systems |
| Character focus | plot or ideas come first | characters advance the plot | similar weight for plot and people | development and interior life matter most | strongly psychological narration |

Measure vocabulary, brevity, and engagement on scales from 0 to 1. A higher brevity score indicates shorter answers. Give each style score a short explanation grounded in the reader's answers. When a reader finishes early, leave unsupported judgments unknown.
